Taste the Flavors of Sarawak

Experience the rich flavors of Sarawak with this iconic laksa. A creamy, spicy coconut broth surrounds tender chicken, succulent prawns, and soft noodles, garnished with fresh herbs for a perfect balance of taste.

Kolo Mee is Sarawak’s favorite noodle dish — springy egg noodles tossed with savory sauce, tender meat, and fresh spring onions. Simple, tasty, unforgettable.

Kek Lapis Sarawak is a vibrant layered cake, baked with butter, eggs, and spices. Sweet, colorful, and perfect for festive celebrations.
